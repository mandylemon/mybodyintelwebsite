Who can benefit from My Body Intelligence?

  • Anyone seeking to improve their body awareness
  • Professionals in high-pressure or stressful environments
  • Athletes, dancers, and physically active individuals
  • Individuals who exercise infrequently but want to feel more comfortable and confident in their bodies

What makes My Body Intelligence unique?

  • Personalized, credible guidance based on your actual practice—not generic advice
  • Curated by your trusted instructors to ensure continuity and reliability
  • AI-powered coaching ensures safe and effective at-home practice.
  • Bridges the gap between in-person instruction and everyday life, ensuring that positive change lasts

How does My Body Intelligence work?

  1. Start with a workshop or class to learn how to recognize your body's signals.
  2. Continue your learning at home with an interactive app that uses AI and your camera to guide your movements and track your progress.
  3. Receive personalized prompts, feedback, and explanations—curated by your instructors—to help you build lasting habits and deeper understanding.
